= Job charging (CHARGE)

When a job completes, is cancelled, fails or reaches its time limit, Slurm runs the Slurm-quota job completion script.
It charges the CPU and GPU time actually consumed by the job to its user/account pair, and removes the preallocation
of the job. The consumption of a user or an account is the sum of the consumption of its pairs.

The charge operations are logged in `/var/log/slurm/charge/slurm-quota-charge.log` on `{controller}`, one line per job:
`charge: user=<user> account=<account> job_id=<id> +<cpu> CPUsecs +<gpu> GPUsecs uuid=<uuid> prealloc_status=<status>`.

// tag::FT-CHARGE-001[]
[[FT-CHARGE-001]]
== FT-CHARGE-001 — Completed job is charged and its preallocation released

Feature:: Job charging
Interface:: Slurm
Objective:: The CPU time consumed by a completed job is charged to its user and account, and its preallocation is
removed.
Prerequisites::
* `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` have no consumption and no preallocation.
Steps::
. As `{user_a}`, submit a job of 2 tasks running for about 2 minutes:
+
[source,console,subs=attributes+]
----
$ sbatch -A {account_a} -p {cpu_partition} -n 2 -t 10 --wrap "sleep 120"
----
. Wait for the job to complete, then get its allocated CPUs and elapsed time:
+
[source,console,subs=attributes+]
----
$ sacct -j <jobid> -X --noheader --format=AllocCPUS,ElapsedRaw
----
. Run `slurm-quota stats --seconds` as `{user_a}`.
. As root on `{controller}`, read the last lines of the charge log.
Expected results::
* `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` consumed CPU time is `AllocCPUS × ElapsedRaw` seconds.
* `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` have no preallocation left.
* The charge log has a line for the job with `user={user_a} account={account_a}`, the same CPU seconds,
`+0 GPUsecs` and `prealloc_status=removed`.
// end::FT-CHARGE-001[]

// tag::FT-CHARGE-002[]
[[FT-CHARGE-002]]
== FT-CHARGE-002 — GPU consumption is charged

Feature:: Job charging
Interface:: Slurm
Objective:: The GPU time consumed by a job is charged from the GPUs allocated to the job.
Prerequisites::
* `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` have no consumption and no preallocation.
Steps::
. As `{user_a}`, submit a job with 2 GPUs of type `{gpu_type_1}` running for about 2 minutes:
+
[source,console,subs=attributes+]
----
$ sbatch -A {account_a} -p {gpu_partition} -n 1 -t 10 --gpus={gpu_type_1}:2 --wrap "sleep 120"
----
. Wait for the job to complete, then get its allocated TRES and elapsed time:
+
[source,console,subs=attributes+]
----
$ sacct -j <jobid> -X --noheader --format=AllocTRES%80,ElapsedRaw
----
. Run `slurm-quota stats --seconds` as `{user_a}`.
Expected results::
* `AllocTRES` contains `gres/gpu:{gpu_type_1}=2`.
* `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` consumed GPU time is `2 × ElapsedRaw` seconds.
* `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` have no GPU preallocation left.
// end::FT-CHARGE-002[]

// tag::FT-CHARGE-003[]
[[FT-CHARGE-003]]
== FT-CHARGE-003 — Cancelled pending job releases its preallocation

Feature:: Job charging
Interface:: Slurm
Objective:: A job cancelled before it starts releases its preallocation and is not charged.
Prerequisites::
* `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` have no consumption and no preallocation.
Steps::
. As `{user_a}`, submit a held job, check its preallocation with `slurm-quota stats`, then cancel it:
+
[source,console,subs=attributes+]
----
$ sbatch -H -A {account_a} -p {cpu_partition} -n 2 -t 10 --wrap "sleep 60"
$ slurm-quota stats
$ scancel <jobid>
----
. Run `slurm-quota stats` as `{user_a}`.
Expected results::
* Before the cancellation, 20 CPU minutes are preallocated to `{user_a}`.
* After the cancellation, `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` have no preallocation and no consumption.
// end::FT-CHARGE-003[]

// tag::FT-CHARGE-004[]
[[FT-CHARGE-004]]
== FT-CHARGE-004 — Cancelled running, failed and timed out jobs are charged

Feature:: Job charging
Interface:: Slurm
Objective:: Jobs ending in other states than completed are charged with the time they actually consumed.
Prerequisites::
* `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` have no consumption and no preallocation.
Steps::
. As `{user_a}`, submit these jobs on `{account_a}` and `{cpu_partition}`, with 1 task each:
.. a job running `sleep 300` with a time limit of 10 minutes, cancelled with `scancel` after about 1 minute of run;
.. a job running `sleep 30; false` with a time limit of 10 minutes;
.. a job running `sleep 600` with a time limit of 1 minute.
. Wait for the end of the jobs, then get their state, allocated CPUs and elapsed time with `sacct`.
. Run `slurm-quota stats --seconds` as `{user_a}`.
Expected results::
* The jobs end in states `CANCELLED`, `FAILED` and `TIMEOUT`.
* The consumed CPU time of `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` is the sum of `AllocCPUS × ElapsedRaw` of the 3 jobs.
* `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` have no preallocation left.
// end::FT-CHARGE-004[]

// tag::FT-CHARGE-005[]
[[FT-CHARGE-005]]
== FT-CHARGE-005 — Job array elements are charged individually

Feature:: Job charging
Interface:: Slurm
Objective:: Each element of a job array is charged when it completes, and the preallocation of the array is reduced by
one element each time.
Prerequisites::
* `{user_a}` and `{account_a}` have no consumption and no preallocation.
* At most 1 job of `{user_a}` can run at the same time, for example with `--array=1-3%1`.
Steps::
. As `{user_a}`, submit an array of 3 elements running one at a time:
+
[source,console,subs=attributes+]
----
$ sbatch -A {account_a} -p {cpu_partition} -n 1 -t 10 --array=1-3%1 --wrap "sleep 60"
----
. After the completion of the first element, run `slurm-quota stats`.
. After the completion of all elements, run `slurm-quota stats --seconds`, and read the charge log on `{controller}`.
Expected results::
* After the first element, the preallocation of `{user_a}` is 20 CPU minutes, with 2 jobs.
* The charge log has a line for each element, with `prealloc_status=decremented` for the first 2 elements and
`prealloc_status=removed` for the last one.
* After all elements, the consumed CPU time is the sum of the consumption of the 3 elements, with no preallocation left.
// end::FT-CHARGE-005[]

// tag::FT-CHARGE-006[]
[[FT-CHARGE-006]]
== FT-CHARGE-006 — Consumption is charged to the user/account pair

Feature:: Job charging
Interface:: CLI
Objective:: Consumption is recorded per user/account pair: a user with jobs on several accounts has the sum of their
consumption, and each account the sum of the consumption of its users.
Prerequisites::
* `{user_a}`, `{user_b}`, `{account_a}` and `{account_b}` have no consumption and no preallocation.
Steps::
. Submit jobs of about 1 minute: one as `{user_a}` on `{account_a}`, one as `{user_a}` on `{account_b}`, one as
`{user_b}` on `{account_a}`. Wait for their completion.
. As `{admin_user}`, run `slurm-quota stats --all --breakdown --seconds`.
Expected results::
* The breakdown of `{user_a}` lists the consumption of `{user_a}` on `{account_a}` and on `{account_b}`, each equal to
the consumption of the matching job, and `{user_a}` consumption is their sum.
* The breakdown of `{account_a}` lists the consumption of `{user_a}` and `{user_b}`, and `{account_a}` consumption is
their sum.
* `{account_b}` consumption is the consumption of the job of `{user_a}` on it.
// end::FT-CHARGE-006[]
